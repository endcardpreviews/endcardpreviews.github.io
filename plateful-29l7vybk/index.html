Plateful

12 interactive Black Friday endcards for Plateful, plus one test variant: 6 for the Titanium Pro Pan and 6 for the Titanium Cookware System, each split 3 pure BFCM (Thanksgiving food imagery) + 3 gifting. Black Friday header, live offer, dynamic date line, free-gift mention and one piece of social proof on every card. Card 11 is the one-tap version; 11B is the earlier four-tap version kept for the click test.

    Shown on a 9:16 phone. Use Rotate to see the landscape version. CTA taps are shown on this page instead of opening the site.Every card reads the viewer’s device date: Black Friday Early Access (Nov 1–26) → Black Friday 11/27 (Today Only) → Black Friday Weekend → Cyber Monday 11/30 → Black Friday Extended (to 12/5) → Limited Time Offer.
