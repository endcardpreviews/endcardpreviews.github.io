Mellow Sleep

5 interactive Black Friday endcards for mellow’s Puff Pillow, built on the same design system as the earlier set. Every card carries the site’s own Black Friday banner with a boxed live countdown, an “as seen in” strip of mellow’s real press logos, and a verbatim five‑star review from a verified buyer. Melbourne and Inter, the indigo wordmark and the cool palette are all from mellowsleep.com.
