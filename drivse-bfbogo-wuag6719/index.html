Drivse

8 interactive endcards for Drivse’s IonDrops 2.0 filtered shower head. Four are gamified and four are benefit-led, as asked. Every card carries the Black Friday offer once, in the bar — Buy 1 Get 2 Free, today only, with the date read off the viewer’s own device clock. The treatment is premium rather than advertorial: the brand’s own Montserrat 700 and Open Sans on a near-black ground with the wordmark’s sage-aqua as the single accent, and the product photographed in chrome against it. The before-and-after is the brand’s own filter cartridge at day 0, two weeks and four weeks — a physical object rather than a claim — and the clarity card is their own side-by-side. Review quotes are verbatim five-star reviews from the brand’s own widget, each with its reviewer’s first name.
