NOBL Travel

11 interactive endcards for NOBL Travel’s Black Friday sale. Every card carries the sale bar across the top in the brand’s deepest green rather than near‑black — the campaign name with the campaign name, “up to 67% off” and the countdown started where it was asked for, 9:30:50. Six carry the brand’s AS SEEN ON strip (Travel+Leisure, Vogue, Forbes, GQ, CNN, lifted from its own hero art); the other four carry the beige LIFETIME WARRANTY / 100‑DAY TRIAL / 1M+ CUSTOMERS row with the site’s own icons. Three catalog cards show four products each with the site’s SAVE badges and price strikethrough, three are the ‘Black Friday’ Bundle 6.0, and two are the site’s own games — the match‑three Black Friday Offer pop-up and the Pick a Card Getaway. Every interaction happens on the thing that was tapped; no card changes a panel at the foot of the frame. Prices come from the live collection JSON and Montserrat is embedded as woff2 in four weights.

    Shown on a 9:16 phone. Use Rotate to see the landscape version.
