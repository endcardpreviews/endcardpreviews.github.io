Willie’s Remedy+

28 interactive Black Friday endcards for Willie’s Remedy+: eight general, five on the Wonderland Bundle, five on Build Your Own, five on the High Road Bundle and five on gifting. Every card carries the site’s Black Friday lockup — 25% off sitewide plus free gifts — and the campaign line is set from the viewer’s own device date. Highway Gothic and Highway Gothic Expanded, navy, cream and Wonderland red are all from willies.com.
